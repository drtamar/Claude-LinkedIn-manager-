## Cross-check (web)
- Exact % stack matches FDA NDC 84896-006 "Bee Venom Skin Firming Cream" by Guangzhou Mizi Biotechnology (5% bee venom, 5% mint essence, 2% cypress, 1% tea tree, 1% borneol, 0.1% panthenol, 0.1% arginine) — a registered OTC drug cream, not this 30 ml Shantou dropper.
- 5% actual Apis mellifera venom is not credible in a cheap cosmetic serum: real cosmetic venom sits at trace levels (0.006–0.1%); 5% would be cytotoxic and require allergy warnings. Same for 5% menthol.
- Conclusion: the % card is a copy of the Mizi drug label used as marketing. Real contents are likely water + glycerin + oils + a whisper of menthol/fragrance.

## Verdict
Lousy / do not rely on. Do not use the claimed % as the formula.
